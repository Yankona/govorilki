# «Мой комикс» — the comic where the child is the hero

Говорилки (a speech, reading and learning game for children 5–10) already has comic series. This one is personal:
the hero is **the child who plays** (drawn as a little superhero with a power the child picked) together with their
friend (a tiger cub, koala, panda, unicorn, footballer… — it varies). The game knows what the child loves (football,
space, ballet…) and builds each story from two parts written by hand:

- a **plot** (a skeleton: quest, mystery, rescue, competition, invention…) — 3 episodes, each ends with a choice the
  next episode remembers;
- a **world** (an interest: football, space, ballet…) — places, the precious thing, a helper, a rival, real facts,
  jokes, magic words.

So «Пропавшая вещь» × «Футбол» is a story about the lost golden ball at the stadium, and the same plot × «Балет» is
about the lost crystal pointe shoe at the theatre. Both must read like a real story, not like fill-in-the-blanks: write
whole, lively lines; a slot is only a noun or a name.

All characters and stories are ORIGINAL: no Marvel/DC/Disney/anime/cartoon characters or look-alikes, no brands, no
real people. Kind humour, no fear, no violence; rivals are funny and end up friends. Facts must be true.

## Three reading levels — every text is an array of three: [L0, L1, L2]
- L0 (just reads syllables, 5–6 y.): 1–4 short common words, max 26 letters AFTER slots are filled (count a slot as
  12 letters; at most one slot in L0), no rare words, ends with «!» «.» or «?».
- L1 (reads short sentences, 7 y.): one sentence of 4–10 words, max 70 characters (slots counted as 14).
- L2 (fluent, 8–10 y.): 1–2 sentences, 9–24 words, max 150 characters (slots counted as 14); richer words, humour.
All three tell the same moment. Use «ё» everywhere.

## Placeholders (the ONLY ones allowed)
- `{name}` — the child's name, **nominative only**, as a subject or when addressed («{name}, смотри!», «{name} бежит.»).
  Never decline it.
- `{a|b}` — the child's gender: boy form | girl form, for past verbs and adjectives about the child:
  «{name} {нашёл|нашла} ключ!», «Ты {готов|готова}?». Nothing else may be gendered about the child.
- `{fr}` — the friend's name, nominative only; never past tense or adjectives about the friend («{fr} смеётся» ✓,
  «{fr} нашёл» ✗).
- World slots (filled by the world; never put a past-tense verb or an adjective that agrees with a slot noun):
  - `{place_in}` «на стадионе», `{place_to}` «на стадион» — the world's main place, with its preposition;
  - `{thing}` / `{thing_acc}` / `{thing_gen}` — the precious thing of the world in nominative / accusative / genitive,
    WITHOUT preposition («Золотой мяч» / «Золотой мяч» / «Золотого мяча»);
  - `{event}` / `{event_to}` / `{event_in}` — the big event: «матч» / «на матч» / «на матче»;
  - `{npc}` — the world's helper (name, nominative only); `{rival}` — the world's rival (name, nominative only);
  - `{crowd}` — the people who care, plural nominative («болельщики»);
  - `{tool}` / `{tool_acc}` — a useful object, nominative / accusative («свисток»).
  Present tense with a slot subject is fine («{thing} исчезает!», «{rival} хохочет»).

## Who is drawn
Panels name who stands in them, left to right, from: `kid` (the child hero), `me` (the friend), `npc`, `rival`;
`sp` = who speaks (`kid`, `me`, `npc`, `rival`) or `nar` (a caption box). Scenes: `home` (the world's main place),
`away` (a second place), `out` (outdoors), or a fixed one: stadium, locker, park, street, city, room, kitchen, garden,
space, pad, moon, mars, lab, jungle, volcano, cave, sea, night, stage, track, castle.
