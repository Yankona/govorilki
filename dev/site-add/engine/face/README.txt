The magic mirror's eyes in Govorilki (the speech exercises): a face model that runs in the browser, on the phone, served
from this site instead of a CDN. The camera picture goes only into this model on the device; nothing is recorded or sent.
Only a few numbers come out of each frame (how open the jaw is, how wide the smile, how round the lips, how puffed the
cheeks). Unchanged copies (Apache License 2.0, https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0, Google LLC):
