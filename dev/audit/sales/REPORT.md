## Recommendation: simplest viable path for the first 100 paying families

**Path: trabalhador independente + merchant of record + the existing PWA; app stores later.**

1. **Legal form.** Open activity as trabalhador independente (free, one afternoon on the Portal). Ask an accountant one question before clicking: CAE (62010/58290 → coefficient 0.35/0.15) vs CIRS 1332 (0.75) for "sale of access to an online educational game" — it changes taxable income by half. Choose IVA art. 53 (≤ €15 000) and the regime simplificado. No company, no mandatory accountant, no organised accounting. Move to a unipessoal only when revenue justifies ~€1 500/yr of overhead or liability becomes a concern.
2. **Payments.** Use a merchant of record so the parent's VAT (19 % DE, 23 % PT…), the consumer invoice, OSS filings, refunds and chargebacks are not yours. Two realistic choices: **Paddle** (one monthly payout, min €100, sole traders accepted) or **Stripe Managed Payments** (Portugal is supported; same ≈ 5 % all-in on EEA cards; you keep Stripe's Checkout/Billing/customer portal and can later turn Managed Payments off and stay on Stripe). Lemon Squeezy is closing; Gumroad is too expensive; Polar is fine but younger. Your only VAT duty becomes: one monthly invoice to the MoR ("IVA – Autoliquidação"), VIES check, declaração recapitulativa — ask the accountant to set this up once.
3. **Offer.** One **family plan** (several child profiles) with an **annual price** (fee ≈ 6–7 % vs ≈ 15 % monthly at €4.99). Trial: free core already exists, so a paid trial that auto-converts is unnecessary — avoid it until the DFA settles; if you keep one, send the reminder before conversion.
4. **Consumer flow** (one checkout page + one account page): price incl. VAT, what is included, renewal terms, a checkbox "start now; I understand I may still withdraw within 14 days and pay for the days used" (service, not content — CJEU C-234/25), a visible "Withdraw from contract here" button and a "Cancel subscription here" button with a confirmation step and e-mail confirmation (covers Art. 11a and §312k BGB), refund policy page, Terms, Privacy policy (parent is the data subject and payer; child profile minimal; consent age 13 PT / 16 DE), Impressum-style seller block (name, address or PO box, e-mail, NIF), Livro de Reclamações link, RAL entity. Have a lawyer read these four pages once.
5. **App stores later.** Keep the stores out of the first 100 families: Apple requires IAP (26 %/15 % in the EU, or 15 %/10 % link-out + Kids Category parental gate + no third-party analytics) and Google requires Play Billing (10 % + 5 % on EEA subscriptions since 2026-06-30) plus Families SDK rules. When demand appears: Google first (TWA + Digital Goods API, $25 once, Families policy already matched by the no-ads/no-tracking design), Apple second ($99/yr, Small Business Program, web subscription honoured in-app only if also offered as IAP).
